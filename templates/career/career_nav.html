Recruitment Scam Warning

We are aware of a scam involving scammers impersonating members of the Lloyds Bureau recruitment team, posting false advertisements on job websites, conducting interviews (often via messaging sites such as Wire) and issuing false employment documents. The scammer may capture the victims' personal information and ask them to transfer money or purchase IT equipment.

Lloyds Bureau does not conduct interviews or contact candidates using messaging apps like Wire or Signal. We will never ask potential or successful candidates to transfer money, provide credit card details or purchase IT equipment for any purpose before starting a position with us.

All of our open positions are listed on our Careers page. If you would like to verify the authenticity of the contact you have had with our recruitment team, please contact us at: people@lloydsbureau.com
