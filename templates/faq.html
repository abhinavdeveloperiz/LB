Your Questions, Answered

Find quick answers to common questions about our certification services.

LB Certifications Pvt. Ltd is an independent, internationally accredited certification body. We specialize in providing auditing and certification services for a wide range of management system standards (such as ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001). Our goal is to help organizations demonstrate their commitment to quality, safety, environmental responsibility, and overall excellence.

We offer a comprehensive portfolio of management system certifications, including:

  • ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management)
  • ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management)
  • ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety)
  • ISO 27001:2022 (Information Security)
  • ISO 22000:2018 (Food Safety Management)

For a complete list of standards we certify, please visit our 'Services' page or contact us directly.

Our certification process is typically structured in the following stages:
  1. Application & Quotation: You submit your application, and we provide a detailed, no-obligation quote.
  2. Stage 1 Audit (Document Review): Our auditors review your management system documentation to check for compliance with the standard.
  3. Stage 2 Audit (Implementation): We conduct an on-site (or remote) audit to verify that your management system is fully implemented and effective.
  4. Certification Decision: Following a successful audit and technical review, we issue your certificate.
  5. Surveillance Audits: We conduct periodic surveillance audits (usually annually) to ensure you maintain compliance.

Most management system certificates (like ISO 9001) are valid for three years.

To maintain the validity of your certificate, you must successfully complete annual surveillance audits. At the end of the three-year cycle, a recertification audit is required to renew your certificate.

Our auditors and decision-makers are bound by confidentiality agreements and are free from any commercial or financial pressures that could influence their findings.
